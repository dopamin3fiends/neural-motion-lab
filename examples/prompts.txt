# Example Prompts

## Character Motion

```
A person walking forward, smooth motion, cinematic lighting
A person turning around, natural movement
A person waving hello, friendly gesture
A person nodding in agreement, subtle motion
A person smiling and looking at camera
```

## Scene Actions

```
A person walking in a beautiful garden, spring day
A person standing in the rain, dramatic lighting
A person dancing, energetic movement, party scene
A person reading a book, peaceful atmosphere
A person drinking coffee, morning scene
```

## Camera Movements

```
Close-up of a person's face, slow zoom in
A person walking away, camera following
Wide shot of a person in a landscape, cinematic
Over-the-shoulder view of a person looking ahead
```

## Styles

```
Cinematic style, film grain, dramatic lighting
Anime style, vibrant colors, smooth animation
Realistic style, natural lighting, 4K quality
Vintage style, retro colors, film aesthetic
```

## Advanced Prompts

```
A person walking through a crowded street, neon lights, cyberpunk style, night scene
A professional portrait shot with a person slowly turning their head, studio lighting, high quality
A person performing a traditional dance, cultural attire, outdoor temple setting, golden hour
```

## Tips

1. Be specific about the motion you want
2. Include style and lighting descriptors
3. Mention the scene context
4. Use quality modifiers (cinematic, high quality, 4K)
5. Specify camera angles if needed
